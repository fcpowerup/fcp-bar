- Improved Finder multi-tab handling

- General stability improvements

## v0.1.1

- In-app update support
- Improved Finder stability

## v0.1.0 beta

First public beta.

- Windows-style taskbar with frosted or liquid glass (liquid glass requires macOS 26+)
- Classic Start menu with browsing of the Applications folder
- Multi-display support
- OLED burn-in protection
- Automatically hides the macOS Dock; restores when you quit
- Installer attachment is always named `FCP-Bar.dmg`: open it and drag FCP Bar into Applications
- Beta builds may still have compatibility or stability issues—feedback welcome
